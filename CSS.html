Brand Story

Allures was founded in 2024 in Ga Matlala, Limpopo, with a dream to create affordable luxury perfumes inspired by African botanicals.What started as a small home business now crafts signature scents for South Africa.

Mission

To provide high-quality, long-lasting fragrances that tell a story of elegance, confidence and African heritage, while keeping eye comfortability with soft colors and quality.

Values

Quality - Elegance - Sustainability - Community. We use cruelty-free ingredients, soft colors for user comfort, and support local farmers for marula and rooibos.
